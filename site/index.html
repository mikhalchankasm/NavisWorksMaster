Tell your AI what to do in Navisworks.

NavisHelper connects Claude, Codex, Cursor and other AI apps to the Navisworks window you have open. Ask in plain words: find items, review clashes, save viewpoints, export a fly-through.

What it does

The coordination work you repeat every day, in one sentence

127 tools behind the scenes; you only describe the result. It also works from the NavisHelper panel, without any AI.

Find and show

Select items by name or property, hide, isolate and color them across a federated model.

Clashes

Review Clash Detective results, group them, isolate a clash, save viewpoints and reports.

Viewpoints and sections

One viewpoint per item, section planes and boxes, markups — saved in the document.

Fly-throughs

Animations from saved viewpoints, exported to PNG, JPG or AVI, ready to compress into MP4.

Reports

Properties and model-tree names to Excel; property audits of the current selection.

How it works

Three steps, no administrator rights

Everything runs on your computer: NavisHelper opens no network ports.

Install

Run NavisHelperSetup.exe with Navisworks closed. The ribbon gets a NavisHelper tab.

Connect your AI app

The installer connects Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others; Claude Desktop takes the .mcpb extension.

Ask

Describe the task. The AI shows each change first and applies it only after you confirm.

Try asking

Plain words, real model

Find all pumps, select them and zoom to them.
Show the active clashes in HVAC vs Structure and isolate the first one.
Make a fly-through along the instrumentation route and export it to video.
Color the model by system, but show me the result first.

Ready-made requests for fly-throughs, section tours and clash walkthroughs, with quality presets: copy and adapt them.

Safety

You stay in control of the model

  • Everything stays localThe AI app talks to Navisworks only on your computer.
  • Preview firstCommands that change the model show what they will do before they do it.
  • Protected modeTurn it on in the panel, and every change waits for your "Allow".
