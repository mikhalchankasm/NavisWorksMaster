New features

From Navisworks to video, FBX and Twinmotion

Three new ways to take the model out of Navisworks: a fly-through along a route, one FBX file per item, and a Datasmith scene for Twinmotion and Unreal Engine.

2.20

Fly-through along a route

The AI sets a viewpoint on every instrument of a route, builds an animation that pauses at each stop and exports PNG or JPG frames or an AVI video. Each view keeps its section planes and markups in the clip.

  • One viewpoint per item: viewpoints_per_item
  • Pauses and smoothing: viewpoint_animation
  • Frames or video in one call: viewpoint_animation_export

Ask the AI“Make a fly-through along the instrument line, pause at each instrument and export a video.”

2.21

FBX export, one file per item

Levels, sections or units, each into its own NNN_name.fbx. Pick the items and a depth: 0 exports the items, 1 their children.

  • Hidden items are skipped; visibility stays as it was
  • Progress and Cancel in the panel and over MCP
  • FBX settings come from the Options Editor

Ask the AI“Export every building level to its own FBX file in D:\Export.”

2.21

Twinmotion and Unreal: Datasmith export

A saved model file becomes a .udatasmith scene for Twinmotion and Unreal Engine. The export runs in a separate hidden Navisworks, so your session keeps working.

  • A button on the panel's Export tab, and an MCP tool
  • Files saved by Navisworks 2027 go through a temporary 2026 copy
  • Cancel ends the hidden processes at once

Ask the AI“Export this model to Datasmith for Twinmotion.”

What it does

The coordination work you repeat every day, in one sentence

133 tools behind the scenes; you only describe the result. It also works from the NavisHelper panel, without any AI.

Find and show

Select items by name or property, hide, isolate and color them across a federated model.

Clashes

Review Clash Detective results, group them, isolate a clash, save viewpoints and reports.

Viewpoints and sections

One viewpoint per item, section planes and boxes, markups — saved in the document.

Fly-throughs

Animations from saved viewpoints, exported to PNG, JPG or AVI, ready to compress into MP4.

Export

FBX per item, Datasmith for Twinmotion and Unreal, NWD 2018 and properties to Excel — from the Export tab or by request.

Reports

Properties and model-tree names to Excel; property audits of the current selection.

How it works

Three steps, no administrator rights

Everything runs on your computer: NavisHelper opens no network ports.

Install

Run NavisHelperSetup.exe with Navisworks closed. The ribbon gets a NavisHelper tab.

Connect your AI app

The installer connects Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others; Claude Desktop takes the .mcpb extension.

Ask

Describe the task. The AI shows each change first and applies it only after you confirm.

Try asking

Plain words, real model

Find all pumps, select them and zoom to them.
Show the active clashes in HVAC vs Structure and isolate the first one.
Make a fly-through along the instrumentation route and export it to video.
Color the model by system, but show me the result first.

Ready-made requests for fly-throughs, section tours and clash walkthroughs, with quality presets: copy and adapt them.

Safety

You stay in control of the model

  • Everything stays localThe AI app talks to Navisworks only on your computer.
  • Preview firstCommands that change the model show what they will do before they do it.
  • Protected modeTurn it on in the panel, and every change waits for your "Allow".
